How to make a preliminary estimate of any product: the FPV drones in a garage example

Step 1. Choose a product that sells in the country
Take something in demand — FPV drones right now. Find the price of the same item in US stores first: a 10-inch drone sells for $529–580, the competitive price of $423 is used; a 7-inch with GPS is $652.99 (GEPRC MOZ7 V2, Pyrodrone). Without the price in the country there is nothing to calculate.

Step 2. Break the product into parts and find each one for sale
From the Alibaba catalog: frame $13.70, flight controller with ESCs $59.00, 4 motors at $10.50, video transmitter $28.98, camera $18.29, battery $38.00. Total $199.97, with 20% duty — $239.96. Every part has a link.

Step 3. Choose equipment for the scale
A garage does not need a line, only workstations: assembly workbench $570, soldering station $29.75, fume extractor $98.60. Two stations — $1,396.70. A factory uses a ready-made line (for example, Sanhok for $199,999).

Step 4. Set the staff and output
The family only sells; 2 hired assemblers build. On the Sanhok line 12 people assemble 100 drones a day — about 1 hour per drone. So 2 people × 8 hours = 16 drones a day.

Step 5. Calculate direct expenses per day
Equipment $1,396.70 ÷ 5 years ÷ 365 = $0.77; wages 2 × $10,000 ÷ 30 = $666.67; rent 0 (own garage). Total $667.43 a day.

Step 6. Unit cost
Parts with duty + direct expenses ÷ output: $239.96 + $667.43 ÷ 16 = $281.68.

Step 7. Profit
(Price in the country − unit cost) × quantity: ($423 − $281.68) × 16 × 30 = $67,834 a month.

Step 8. Check d
Direct expenses per drone ($41.72) are 17.4% of the parts cost — within 5–40%, so direct expenses do not inflate the unit cost.

Step 9. Module: what else can be made at the same place
The same benches, soldering stations and people. Switch to a 7-inch frame ($11.20) and add GPS ($8.59). Unit cost $288.99, US price $652.99, profit $174,722 a month.

Step 10. What to confirm before starting
This is an estimate. Before starting, check prices and discounts with sellers, real assembly time, shipping, taxes, drone sales rules and sales channels — they are not in the calculation yet.

Any product is estimated with the same steps: price in the country → parts → equipment → staff and output → direct expenses → unit cost → profit → d check.
