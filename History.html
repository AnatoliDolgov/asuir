История создания проекта

The history of the Technology Matrix begins in 2008, when the crisis phenomena that engulfed the world economy became noticeable, followed by the need to understand the root causes of the crisis, without the resolution of which the crisis will continue and intensify. During this period, the so-called "triune task" was formulated, without the solution of which neither the resolution of the crisis nor the so-called import substitution is possible

(1) Ensuring the cost of domestic products at the level of competitors;
(2) ensuring the design of technologies and millions of products (obsolete within 1 - 6 months) and equipment for their production;
(3) Ensuring the production of millions of products that become obsolete within 1 to 6 months - this is only possible if the equipment is able to quickly change over to a new type of product;

The simultaneous fulfillment of these conditions constitutes the corridor in which any innovative program capable of ensuring competition should be situated. The task is moved into the category of archiproblem, due to the need to renew products every 1-6 months, due to their obsolescence, under the conditions of the need to level the advantage of China in the number of engineering and technical specialists in comparison with the USA and Russia by 13 and 100 times respectively.

At the end of 2011, the Presidential Administration proposed a production model with production costs equal to those of competitors, including Chinese and Third World countries. At that time it became clear that the new program "Strategy 2020" (currently "Innovative Russia 2020") will not work. The search for ways to solve the remaining two problems continued.

In mid-2012, discussions were held on how to solve the current problems with the leadership of the Socrates system. Project Socrates (Automated Innovation System) was initiated in 1983 by President Reagan's Administration at the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) level of the U.S. Department of Defense. The goals of the project, created under the leadership of Michael Secora, a physicist by training, were to outpace Japan and all other competitors. This has provided an impressive economic growth rate all 8 years of the first generation of Socrates technology in government projects - average annual GDP growth of 3.8%, the creation of 20 million new jobs, including 82% of highly skilled and therefore highly paid, the fall in unemployment to 5.5%). The current 3rd version of the Sokrat system is designed to provide a technological breakaway from Russia, China and other countries, in the long term, for several generations. To this end, the U.S. is now intensively integrating the Socrates technology generation system at all levels and in all states.

The experience of communicating with the developers of the American system "Socrates" has shown that the system "Socrates" does provide the generation of competitive technologies. Nevertheless, the use of the Socrates system alone is not enough to provide a technological breakaway from certain countries, and even less so to ensure competition, for example, with China. The technology can be copied by competitors, using it in the future to produce products, but at a lower cost.

Nevertheless, in 3-4 quarters of 2012 it was decided to integrate the Socrates system and the Technology Matrix into one system with the beginning of its implementation in late 2012 - early 2013. A series of videoconferences were held at the leadership level of "Business Russia" and the Sokrat system. Due to certain circumstances, this decision had to be subsequently abandoned, with the simultaneous addition of technologies and functions of the Socrates system to the Matrix.

The project provides for the phased deployment of the Technology Matrix, providing public access to the means of automating the design and launch into production of a wide range of goods and related equipment.
