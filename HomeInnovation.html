    операция "Вперед"

From idea to product

The main objective of the project "Technology Matrix" is to provide maximum automation of processes, starting from the creation of new technologies, the design of new products, appropriate equipment and ending with the production itself, while achieving both a competitive technological advantage and the cost of production at the level of the best examples.

The project allows you to solve the so-called "triple problem" without a solution, all components of which is difficult to talk about re - industrialization or import substitution. We mean the need to ensure: the cost of domestic products at the level of competitors; design technology and millions of product names (obsolete in 1 - 6 months) and equipment for their production; and production of millions of product names - it is possible only if the equipment is able to quickly changeover to a new type of product;

The simultaneous fulfillment of these conditions constitutes the corridor in which any innovative program capable of ensuring competition should be situated. The task is moved to the category of archiproblem, due to the need to renew products every 1-6 months, with the search for ways to level the advantage of China in the number of engineering and technical specialists (for the USA and Russia respectively by 13 and 100 times).

The project, even at the initial stage, allows you to lay the foundation for automation of most processes in the creation of new technologies and in the design of new products and the corresponding equipment for its production, with the provision in the future accelerated output in Production, with the rapid retooling of equipment for one or another product in accordance with market requirements.

The closest competitor to one of the subsystems of the Technology Matrix (the subsystem "Ensuring Competitive Advantage") can only be the American system of technology generation - "Socrates" (now the 3rd version, the 1st version was developed in 1983). However, unlike the "Socrates" system, the "Ensuring Competitive Advantage" subsystem is able to produce the necessary solutions in all strategies at once.
