<p>Welcome! Gemini is an automated AI system that is ready to use and designed for developing technologies, products, equipment and production modules. It is intended to facilitate the retooling of equipment for new product types.</p>

<p>The economic algorithms of the Gemini ASUI platform are identical everywhere, allowing for the establishment of production facilities in any country that ensure:</p>
<p><b>Prices of manufactured goods</b> as in Bangladesh + <b>workers' salaries:</b> $10,000 per month : $100,000–$1,000,000 per month;</p>
