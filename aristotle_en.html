Aristotle — technology generator

Technology intelligence based on the “Socrates” methodology (M. Secora): Aristotle establishes which technologies competitors use and what their achievements are, compares them with our own technology (if we make the product ourselves) and, by means of technology maneuvers, finds how to neutralize the competitors’ achievements — so that customers buy from us.

1. Task

The product or component on which competitors must be surpassed.

2. AI analysis

Press “Request for AI”, paste the text into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then paste the AI’s answer into the field below and press “Parse answer”.

3. Which technologies competitors use

Check by another AI

An analysis by one AI may contain errors. Press “Request for check”, paste it into another AI (if the analysis was made by Claude — into ChatGPT or Gemini), and paste the answer below. Statements confirmed by the independent check are marked ✓; doubtful ones ?; wrong ones ✗ with a correction. Several AIs can check in turn — the marks accumulate.

4. How to neutralize the competitors’ achievements

Solutions obtained by technology maneuvers from the comparison. Mark ✓ those you will implement.

Maneuvers from verified conclusions

When the comparison and conclusions have been checked and corrected (section 3), the solutions should be derived again from the verified conclusions. Press the button, paste the request into an AI, paste the answer below. The comparison and conclusions do not change; only branches and solutions do.

5. Partners and transfer to production

The product is created together with partners — institutes and enterprises: development, prototype, tests, pilot batch. The result is the input data for serial production in ASUIR Production.

The button opens ASUIR Production with the fields already filled in: product, equipment, output, workers, shop costs, components, price.

AI connection (optional) — analysis and check with one button, without copying between sites

Access keys (API keys) are stored only in this browser and are sent only directly to Anthropic and OpenAI. Billing is by your key’s plan. If both AIs are connected, one does the analysis and the other the check.

5. Repository of tasks and solutions

Tasks and solutions accumulate in the repository. A record is private by default — only the author sees it; once published, everyone sees it. A complex task is decomposed into components; a solution found can be turned into a new task. This is how the task tree grows in the repository.


Decomposition into components

Components are simpler tasks that are all needed together. The AI proposes a decomposition, the author edits it: one component per line.

How the solutions were obtained (for the specialist): 84 branches of technology maneuvers

After the comparison conclusions, the AI goes through 84 branches: 21 technology maneuvers of “Socrates” (offensive, defensive, technology acquisition, implementation) in each of 4 dimensions — structure, capability, technology flow, time. Each cell answers its own question. Cells can be checked and filled in manually (✎). The list of maneuvers with examples is in the maneuver library.
