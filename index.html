  • Introduction

Welcome to ASUIR Gemini, a ready-to-use system for creating ultra-efficient manufacturing operations with production costs at the level of Bangladesh and staff salaries ranging from $10,000 to $100,000 per month.

Meanwhile, the salary of an entrepreneur, which could be that of an average homemaker—ranges from $100,000 to $1,000,000 per month.

The ASUIR Gemini platform's economic algorithms are identical everywhere, enabling the establishment of production facilities in any country that offer:

Prices of manufactured goods at the level of Bangladesh + workers' salaries: of $10,000 per month Entrepreneur’s income : of $100,000–$1,000,000 per month;

At the same time, the entrepreneur does not need to work personally. – The calculations include wages for hired staff at a rate of $10,000 per person per month;

The algorithms underlying the Gemini ASUIR platform were reviewed by the Russian Ministry of Economic Development in 2011. President D. A. Medvedev then recommended that relevant expert groups consider them to develop a unified position on the formation of Russia’s economic strategy (Ref. OG-D19-2366, December 9, 2011).

Subsequently, the Gemini ASUIR digital platform was refined further to support the strategic provisions of the UN’s 2030 Agenda while implementing all 17 Sustainable Development Goals declared in UN documents.

The standard of living increases with the widespread use of production facilities designed using the Gemini digital platform—for example, by an average of 1,060% in Russia, 934% in China, up to 5,770% in Bangladesh, and 400% in the United States (with an average salary of $5,000, achieved by doubling it while reducing the cost of production and services—and thus prices—to the level seen in Bangladesh).

The Gemini ASUIR platform was certified in 2025. The certificate was signed by ASI CEO S. V. Chupsheva and Roscongress Foundation Director A. A. Stuglev.

Competitive Advantages of the Gemini ASUIR Platform — It may sound paradoxical, but the Gemini ASUIR platform currently has no direct competitors.
