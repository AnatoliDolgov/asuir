  • Introduction

Products manufactured in the US are at least 2.5 times cheaper than those made in China, and there is no need to protect the market with tariffs and duties.

Welcome to ASUIR — a technology that levels the standard of living across all countries by ensuring production costs are at the same level as in Bangladesh and wages are at the same level as in the US or higher.

The economic algorithms of the ASUIR platform are identical everywhere, enabling production systems to be created for any country that ensure:

Prices of manufactured goods at the level of Bangladesh + workers' salaries: at the level of Switzerland and Washington, D.C.Entrepreneur’s income : of $100,000–$1,000,000 per month;

At the same time, the entrepreneur does not need to work personally. –calculations include monthly wages for hired staff at levels typical in Switzerland and the District of Columbia (U.S.);

Production with the ability to retool equipment for a new product type involves setting up a new production line using existing equipment to manufacture related products. Once you have set up a production line, you can use the reconfiguration module to adapt it to produce new products;

The ASUIR system is just as effective for services, achieving identical metrics. It operates with service costs at the level of Bangladesh and staff salaries at the level of Switzerland and the District of Columbia, all while ensuring maximum business profits. As an example, the organization of services at a large hospital in Los -Angeles (ASUIR Service) is shown.

The algorithms underlying the ASUIR platform were reviewed by the Russian Ministry of Economic Development in 2011. President D. A. Medvedev then recommended that relevant expert groups consider them to develop a unified position on the formation of Russia’s economic strategy (Ref. OG-D19-2366, December 9, 2011).

Subsequently, the ASUIR digital platform was refined further to support the strategic provisions of the UN’s 2030 Agenda while implementing all 17 Sustainable Development Goals declared in UN documents.

The ASUIR platform was certified in 2025. The certificate was signed by ASI CEO S. V. Chupsheva and Roscongress Foundation Director A. A. Stuglev.

Competitive Advantages of the ASUIR Platform — It may sound paradoxical, but the ASUIR platform currently has no direct competitors.

Is it possible to develop production processes and services without an ASUIR system? Yes, but this is usually achieved through trial and error, which is time-consuming and problematic. While it is theoretically possible to compare different production processes to select the most efficient one, it is difficult to implement in practice. With ASUIR, however, this can be done in a matter of minutes.
