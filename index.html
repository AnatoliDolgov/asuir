Welcome! The Gemini ASUIR is a ready-to-use automated AI system for developing technologies, products, equipment and production modules. It also facilitates the retooling of equipment for new product types.

The main advantage of the Gemini Automated Production Management System is its ability to create highly efficient manufacturing operations. Production costs are consistently comparable to those in Bangladesh, with staff salaries amounting to $10,000 per month.

The salary of an entrepreneur, who could be a stay-at-home parent, ranges from $100,000 to $1,000,000 per month.

The Gemini ASUIR platform's economic algorithms are identical across the board, enabling production plans to be generated for any country that ensure:

Prices of manufactured goods Prices of manufactured goods as in Bangladesh + workers' salaries: $10,000 per month : $100,000–$1,000,000 per month;

At the same time, the entrepreneur does not need to carry out the work themselves. – The calculations assume that each employee will receive a monthly salary of $10,000.;

The platform's target audience and implementation area.

Owners and developers of technologies, projects, products, and equipment; as well as manufacturers, who may include both ordinary citizens and specialists—workers, engineers, and scientists—and, of course, small, medium, and large enterprises.

A key advantage of the GEMINI ASUIR is the reduction of production costs (and thus prices) for goods and services to the level of Bangladesh, while simultaneously leveling the impact of production expenses, including wages—which can be at Western levels—with a 400–5,800 percent increase in the standard of living.

With widespread use of the ASUI digital platform, the standard of living in each country increases, for example, by an average of 1,060% in Russia, 934% in China, up to 5,770% in Bangladesh, and 400% in the U.S.

How does this work in any country?

When using the GEMINI ASUIR platform on a global scale, enterprises in any country will be able to produce identical goods and services at virtually the same minimum cost (regardless of infrastructure, climate, logistics, or level of development), with a standard of living higher than the current level in any Western country. If the situation develops according to this scenario, important consequences will ensue:

  • all countries will produce goods that are virtually identical in price, the quantity of which will be determined solely by demand;

  • wages in all countries will tend toward an equilibrium level, with an upward trend. In turn, income equality in all countries—as they tend toward equilibrium—eliminates the need for migration in search of a better life.
