ASUI – a ready-to-use, automated platform for eliminating poverty in all countries

In practical terms, the ASUIR achieves several things:

  • ensures exponential economic growth (up to $500,000/month) for households to the level of a dollar millionaire within 2–12 months, through the sale of finished products;

  • On a global scale, it eliminates poverty in all countries by leveling out direct production costs (including wages) in the cost of production, thereby reducing the cost of production to the level of Bangladesh and increasing wages to the level of Switzerland, while ensuring a standard of living for the population that is guaranteed to be higher than the current level in any Western country;

  • equalizes the standard of living (wages, cost of goods) in all countries by ensuring identical production costs and wages;

How does this work in any country?

When using the ASUI platform on a global scale, enterprises in any country will be able to produce identical goods and services at virtually the same minimum cost (regardless of infrastructure, climate, logistics, or level of development), with a standard of living higher than the current level in any Western country. If the situation develops according to this scenario, important consequences will ensue:

  • all countries will produce goods that are virtually identical in price, the quantity of which will be determined solely by demand;

  • wages in all countries will tend toward an equilibrium level, with an upward trend. In turn, income equality in all countries—as they tend toward equilibrium— eliminates the need for migration in search of a better life.
