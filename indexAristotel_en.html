Summary information

The history of the Aristotle project began at the end of 2012 after I received an official invitation from the management of the Socrates technology generation system to collaborate on the creation of an automated innovation management system for the US and Russia at the Quadrigy and Delovaya Rossiya level. Quadrigy and Delovaya Rossiya is perceived and considered by us as a significant opportunity to strengthen the response of the US and Russia to the current economic crisis by proposing, as a key element of the developing Automated Innovation Management System of Russia, an improved version of Socrates.

In the US, Socrates, in addition to its successful use in the corporate environment of the US, with the support of the top 15 (Fortune 15) largest companies, such as General Electric, Ford, IBM, General Motors, and others, is also used at the federal level and at the state level, using as sources of technology both internal resources (organizations, staff, states) and external resources based on alliances (organizations, states, countries).

In 2013, corresponding teleconferences were held at the appropriate level, demonstrating the capabilities of the Socrates system. The entire second half of the year was spent preparing for the integration of the Socrates system in Russia. The Russian Ministry of Defense expressed interest in the Socrates system. However, at the end of 2013, it became clear that due to the location of the Socrates system servers in the United States, there could be problems with integrating the system in Russia. It was suggested that the functions of the Socrates system, adapted for the ASUIR, be implemented independently.

The goals of the Aristotle project. The Aristotle project was created based on the observation that the competitive advantages of a company or country stem primarily from the identification of technologies that are key to solving relevant tasks, whether it is meeting current and future consumer needs or ensuring the country's defense capability in the present and future.

Based on this Aristotle was created to provide a technological advantage, with a complete set of technological strategies for achieving maximum competitive advantages in obtaining and using technologies, while solving a wide range of tasks in all sectors, private and public, where the most important task is to ensure an advantage over competitors.

The main focus of the Aristotle project is the generation of technologies, followed by their effective use, with the help of the ASUIR system in product development and production organization, leveling the advantages of countries with low production costs, such as Bangladesh, as well as the advantages of Western countries in terms of the standard of living of the population, while ensuring high speed of project development and organization of production, with the aim of leveling the advantage of China in the number of technical specialists.

At the same time, unlike the Socrates system, the assessment of competitive advantages in the Aristotle system is carried out immediately for all strategies simultaneously. The functions offered for implementation in the project give system participants a very accurate idea of the resources and vulnerabilities of their competitors, allowing them to determine their potential strategies and the availability of resources to implement these strategies, with the ultimate goal of staying ahead of their competitors.

The result is the ability not only to generate new technologies that meet the goals of organizations, scientific communities, industry, and government, but also to develop symbiotic technology strategies.

"The symbiotic aspects of technology strategies will allow organizations to use each other's technology to create a competitive advantage that they could not create acting alone. The result will be the use of technologies throughout Russia in a highly coordinated but flexible and independent manner, providing competitive advantages both for Russia as a whole and for all its organizations. Moreover, since the use of technology is the basis of all competitive advantage, if Russia's technologies are used in a coordinated manner, the result will be that all of Russia's other resources—labor, money, natural resources, etc.—are also used in a highly coordinated, but flexible and independent manner, further increasing the competitive advantage of Russia and all its private and public organizations.

In other words, the application of symbiotic strategies can ensure the “explosive” development of the country, due to the fact that each new technology, due to its accessibility to all participants in the innovation process, leads to the emergence of new and new products of the innovation process — technologies and products, which in turn also become accessible to all participants in the innovation process.

The Aristotle project – a technology generator – has been developed algorithmically (in 2013, the project description was presented to the Russian Presidential Administration)

As already mentioned, the Aristotle Project has been fully developed algorithmically, and is intended to work in conjunction with the ASUIR system - Aristotle provides technology generation, while ASUIR provides technology implementation in projects and production.

Project participants can include engineers, researchers, developers, design bureaus, institutes, as well as state institutions and the government, with the aim of optimizing management. In the US, for example, the prototype - the “Socrates” project, in addition to successful use in the US corporate environment, with the support of the top 15 (Fortune 15) largest companies, such as General Electric, Ford, IBM, General Motors, etc., is also used at federal and state levels, using both internal resources (organizations, states, countries) and external resources based on alliances (organizations, states, countries) as sources of technology. Any combinations of links to a particular base are acceptable when deploying the system, depending on what works best for the country's economic system.

The project can be used for any region of the world.
