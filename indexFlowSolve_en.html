1. FlowSolve is a self-learning artificial intelligence that responds to challenges and ensures sustainable development.

FlowSolve is a self-learning, recursive intelligence that continuously progresses on any task, fundamentally differing from other AI systems such as GPT, expert systems, and ML models, which do not learn from data, but building thinking from an ideal. FlowSolve — learns from problems, refining its architecture and initiating the transition to a new level while maintaining stability.

In terms of implementation, FlowSolve is a universal thinking interface that allows a person to work with AI to solve any task using a recursive approach to the ideal solution. FlowSolve turns a problem into a stream of solutions that can be transformed into the ideal result or method for solving the task. In this sense, FlowSolve is not just method, but a new way of thinking that is accessible to everyone. The method has been used by the author for approximately 50 years, having undergone many years of testing in this sense when solving complex problems, especially where there are no ready-made solutions. Each option is not just a choice, but a new subproblem with its own ideal. For each one, a new cycle of approximation is started. Recursion continues until a solution is found that is as close as possible to the original ideal, taking into account reality.

2. FlowSolve - a complete cybernetic architecture for managing society

FlowSolve as a universal model for managing human society, where every function — from economics to security — works on the principle of stabilizing the system and transitioning to a new level, excluding destruction, degradation, and conflict.

The use of FlowSolve as a tool for managing human society is based on the natural continuation of architectural principles that exist in nature and technology. At the same time, the universal triad of any complex system, including ourselves, contains three basic elements that ensure decision-making followed by stabilization of the system itself on the path to achieving a particular task:

  • Feedback subsystem (sensors) — evaluates the state of the environment: incoming signals, threats, opportunities.

    • in humans: sight, hearing, touch, proprioception.

    • In a rocket: gyroscopes, GPS, radar, telemetry.

  • Decision-making subsystem (brain/controller) — analyzes signals, compares them with targets, selects an action.

    • in humans: brain, consciousness, intuition.

    • In a rocket: control computer, navigation algorithms.

  • Position correction subsystem (actuators) — implements the solution: movement, course change, reaction.

    • in humans: muscles, joints, voice.

    • in a rocket: rudders, engines, stabilizers.

For humans, disturbances in the environment (e.g., a machine rushing toward a person car rushing towards a person), which can lead to an imbalance in the system (the person) , or its destruction, are transmitted via feedback to the decision-making center (the brain), followed by a correction of the position, with the person moving out of the way of the impact – all in automatic mode.

Human society is also a complex system and therefore must work in a similar way: identify threats, make decisions, implement corrections, and do so automatically.

In this sense, FlowSolve is a multi-level subsystem of strategic planning, with decision-making that promotes sustainability, adaptation, and preservation of the integrity society as a dynamic system, for which the main thing is the system itself, in this case society, its sustainability and integrity, with a response to environmental disturbances through a feedback subsystem, followed by compensation for these disturbances if they worsen the state of society, transforming society into an adaptive, self-regulating system in which social problems become the primary impulse, and the entire architecture works as an automatic adaptive system, responding to real signals from the environment.
