Summary information

The goals of the NEP project – Russia's new economic policy.

The NEP project formula, which works in any country (Russia, the US, Bangladesh, Kenya, Germany – it's the same everywhere):

Prices at the level of Bangladesh + Salaries at the level of the US = Standard of living like in the US (or higher)

With the widespread use of NEP, the standard of living in each country will increase, for example, in China by 438%, in Bangladesh by up to 3025%, in Russia by 1200% and in the US by 900% — in the US, due to the reduction of production costs to the level of Bangladesh.

The algorithms underlying the NEP project were reviewed by the Russian Ministry of Economic Development in 2011, with a subsequent recommendation, on the initiative of President D. A. Medvedev, for consideration by relevant expert groups to develop a unified position on the formation of Russia's economic strategy (ref. OG-D19-2366 dated 09.12.2011).

I would like to note that the algorithms of the NEP project fully correspond to the laws of economics, with the results described above, as well as in the documentation.

Objectives of the NEP project. The project provides for both the leveling of the advantages of countries with low production costs, such as Bangladesh, as well as the advantages of Western countries in terms of the standard of living of the population, while ensuring a high speed of project development and production organization in order to level the advantage of China in the number of technical specialists.

When using NEP on a global scale, enterprises in any country will be able to produce identical goods with virtually the same minimum cost (regardless of infrastructure, climate, logistics, level of development), with salaries 30 times higher than the current level. At the same time, if, for example, salaries in Bangladesh increase 30 times (3000%) to the US level, the cost of products manufactured in Bangladesh will change by only 9%. This means an increase in the standard of living in the country to the level of the United States. If this scenario develops, there will be important consequences:

  • all countries will produce goods that are virtually identical in price, the quantity of which will be determined only by domestic demand;

  • wages in all countries will tend toward an equilibrium value, with a tendency to grow.

Explanation: The standard of living was calculated based on the availability of basic consumer goods under the new structure cost and income structure. With the introduction of the NEP, the cost of production will be standardized to the minimum level, and wages will be brought up to the US level and above, ensuring a sharp increase in the purchasing power of the population in all countries, regardless of their current economic situation.

The ability to ensure, in any country, including Russia, production with wages at the US level and production costs at the minimum level, in fact, transforms the NEP into a new economic model that breaks with old economic models, making geographical location irrelevant to production costs, eliminating the need to search for cheap labor, reducing migration, and simultaneously ensuring political invulnerability.

At the same time, if we are talking about leveling the advantage of Western countries in terms of the standard of living of the population, then we are talking first and foremost about raising the wages of Russians involved in the production of various goods to the level of the leading Western countries, or better, based on the understanding that all other benefits of civilization are derivatives of this category of production costs.

In terms of leveling the advantage of countries with low production costs, the NEP system provides virtually equal cost of goods for identical production, for example, in Russia, Kazakhstan, China, Germany — any country, with a difference in production costs, including wages, of 30 times or more — the cost of production remains practically the same low.

In summary, it can be argued that NEP is not only an economic model, but also a mechanism capable of eliminating global imbalances, creating a fair economy, ensuring explosive growth through symbiotic production, and rebuild social relations by changing living standards. At the same time, wage growth, lower production costs, and access to production change motivation, eliminate tension, and form new forms of cooperation.

The competitive advantages of the project — it sounds paradoxical, but at the moment, the NEP seems to have no direct competitors.

Descriptions of ASUIR, NEP, and Aristotle—interrelated projects— were prepared for presentation to M.V.
